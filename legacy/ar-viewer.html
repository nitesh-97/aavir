📱 How to use AR

  • Open this page on a mobile device (AR is not supported on desktop).
  • Tap "View in AR".
  • Android: Uses Google's Scene Viewer — point camera at a flat surface and tap to place.
  • iOS: Uses AR Quick Look — point camera and tap to place.
  • Move: Drag the object with one finger.
  • Rotate: Use two fingers to rotate.
  • Scale: Pinch with two fingers.
